# Future Ideas

[FUTURE] Most Wanted wall and tracker
Create a police station wall or MDT page showing Most Wanted suspects. Each suspect should have a name, photo/icon, wanted reason, last known area, and reward. If the player finds or arrests them during normal patrol, they receive a bonus.

[FUTURE] Promotion board / supervisor review
When the player becomes eligible for promotion, do not auto-promote. Instead, require a supervisor review, promotion board, or approval event.

[FUTURE] Discipline system
Track bad actions, failed calls, unnecessary force, missed calls, or improper behavior. Discipline should affect promotion eligibility.

[FUTURE] Shift summary screen
At the end of each shift, show calls cleared, XP earned, whether the shift counted, and current promotion progress.

[FUTURE] More MDT tabs
Possible future tabs:
- Calls
- Career
- Reports
- Wanted
- Map
- Warrants
- Vehicles
- Personnel

[FUTURE] Report writing
After certain calls, require a short report or checklist. Better reports can improve performance score.

[FUTURE] Call grading
Calls can be graded based on response, correct location, contact made, proper resolution, and whether the player cleared correctly.

[FUTURE] Rank-gated features
Higher ranks can unlock new responsibilities, call types, equipment access, supervisor tasks, or specialized units.

[FUTURE] Time-in-rank requirement
Promotion should require time served at the current rank, not just XP.
