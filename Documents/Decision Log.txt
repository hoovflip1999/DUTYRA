# Decision Log

STATUS LEGEND
[IN GAME] = Working and committed
[IN PROGRESS] = Built/tested but may need more polish later
[FUTURE] = Planned idea, not built yet

[IN GAME] Radio wheel system
The player uses Q to open a mouse-controlled radio wheel. The wheel handles 10-41, 10-42, 10-76, 10-97, and 10-8 actions. Old direct R call acceptance was removed.

[IN GAME] Duty status system
The player can go on duty with 10-41 and off duty with 10-42. Duty status changes control dispatch calls, HUD status, and availability.

[IN GAME] Dispatch call flow
Dispatch assigns calls while the player is on duty. Calls currently include Suspicious Person at the gas station and Noise Complaint at the apartment.

[IN GAME] Call status progression
Calls move through Pending, En Route, On Scene, Objective Complete, and Cleared states.

[IN GAME] Call response areas
The player must be at the correct call location before marking on scene or clearing the call.

[IN GAME] Subject contact system
The player must contact the involved person before clearing the call. Contacting the subject adds call-specific results.

[IN GAME] Subject dialogue popup
Subjects/residents show short dialogue when contacted.

[IN GAME] Call cleared popup
Clearing a call shows a result popup with the call title, result note, performance XP, and available status.

[IN GAME] Laptop-style MDT
The MDT now opens as a large laptop/computer-style interface instead of a small text box. It uses clickable mouse tabs.

[IN GAME] MDT Calls tab
The Calls tab shows current call information, location, priority, status, notes, and next action.

[IN GAME] MDT Career tab
The Career tab shows current rank, current shift progress, performance XP, calls cleared, shifts completed, promotion eligibility, and promotion status.

[IN GAME] Performance XP foundation
Calls award Performance XP. XP does not automatically promote the player. It only contributes to future promotion eligibility.

[IN GAME] Temporary XP progress popup
When XP is earned, a small progress popup appears briefly on the right side and then disappears. XP is not always shown on the normal HUD.

[IN GAME] Real shift completion progress
Going 10-41 starts a shift. Clearing at least one call during the shift makes the shift eligible to count. Going 10-42 completes the shift only if the required call count was met.

[DECISION] Promotions are not automatic
Promotion will require more than XP. Future promotion should depend on XP, calls cleared, shifts completed, evaluations, discipline history, rank requirements, and possibly promotion boards.

[FUTURE] Shift end summary
Add a popup when going 10-42 showing whether the shift counted or did not count.

[FUTURE] Most Wanted system
Add a Most Wanted wall/tracker. Wanted people can exist in the city, and finding/arresting them gives extra rewards or bonuses.
