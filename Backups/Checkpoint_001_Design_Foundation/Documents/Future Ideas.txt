# Future Ideas

## SWAT Career Path

Idea:
Allow the player to apply for SWAT after gaining experience, passing qualifications, and meeting department requirements.

Why Later:
SWAT requires advanced AI, tactics, equipment, team commands, and high-risk scenarios. Patrol must work first.

---

## K-9 Unit

Idea:
Allow the player to become a K-9 officer. The dog stays at the station and is checked out during duty.

Why Later:
K-9 gameplay requires tracking, scent systems, dog AI, suspect reactions, and special call types.

---

## Detective Career

Idea:
Allow the player to become a detective and handle longer investigations.

Why Later:
Detective gameplay requires case management, evidence linking, interviews, warrants, and prosecutor review.

---

## Multiple Departments

Idea:
Allow transfers between city police, sheriff’s office, state police, and other agencies within the same open world.

Why Later:
The first playable version should focus on one department and patrol first.

---

## Large Open World Expansion

Idea:
Expand the map into city, suburbs, highway, rural county, parks, and industrial areas.

Why Later:
The first version should use one small district to keep performance and workload manageable.

---

## Major City Events

Idea:
Add events such as storms, parades, concerts, elections, sports games, and emergencies.

Why Later:
These should come after the basic patrol and dispatch systems work.

---

## Advanced Criminal Organizations

Idea:
Add gangs, organized crime, repeat offenders, and criminals who adapt to police behavior.

Why Later:
This requires the living city and memory systems to be working first.

---

## Prosecutor Review System

Idea:
Cases can be reviewed by a prosecutor. Evidence may be accepted, rejected, or dismissed based on officer actions.

Why Later:
This is important, but it should come after reports, evidence, and arrest systems are functional.

---

## Officer Relationships

Idea:
Important NPC officers have personalities, strengths, weaknesses, and relationships with the player.

Why Later:
This adds depth, but patrol gameplay must work first.

---

## Home Buying

Idea:
Allow the player to buy different homes as their career progresses.

Why Later:
This supports career immersion but is not required for the first playable patrol version.
