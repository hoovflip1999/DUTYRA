# Police Simulator

## Vision

Create the most immersive police career simulator possible.

The game should feel like living the life of a police officer, not just completing missions.

Every shift should create new stories based on a living world where citizens, criminals, and officers all have their own lives and make their own decisions.

The player's choices should matter. There should rarely be one "correct" solution to a situation.

## Core Principles

* The world continues even if the player does nothing.
* Citizens have daily routines.
* Criminals adapt to police actions.
* Every traffic stop should feel unique.
* Every call for service can have multiple outcomes.
* Simple controls with deep gameplay.
* Player decisions affect reputation, career, and the city.
* Realistic police procedures without overwhelming the player.

## Long-Term Goal

Build a police simulation that players can enjoy for hundreds of hours because the city constantly creates new situations instead of repeating scripted missions.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Development Philosophy

This game is not mission-based.

It is a living city simulation where the player experiences life as a police officer.

Crimes are created by the simulation, not by scripted missions.

The city exists whether the player is watching or not.

The player responds to the city's events instead of triggering them.

Every system should ask one question:

"What would naturally happen next?"

## What Makes This Game Different?

This is not a mission-based police game.

This is a living police career simulator.

The player is not the center of the world.

The city continues to function whether the player is present or not.

Citizens have lives, jobs, families, routines, and relationships.

Crimes happen because of events inside the simulation, not because the game randomly creates missions.

The player's actions influence the city, their career, public trust, and future events.

The goal is to create unique stories that no two players will experience in exactly the same way.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Citizen System

Every citizen is a persistent character.

Citizens are never just random NPCs.

Each citizen should have:

* First name
* Last name
* Age
* Gender
* Home
* Job
* Daily schedule
* Vehicle
* Driver's license status
* Criminal history
* Family members
* Friends
* Personality
* Mood
* Stress level
* Health
* Financial status

Citizens remember previous interactions with the player.

The same citizen can appear again weeks or months later in the game.

Their life continues even when the player is not interacting with them.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## Career Progression

The player begins as a police recruit.

The player builds a career through performance instead of experience points.

Promotions should be earned through:

* Good decision making
* Successful investigations
* Professional conduct
* Positive evaluations
* Department training
* Time in service

The player can specialize in different career paths instead of following one fixed path.

The player's career should feel personal, with different players reaching different positions based on how they choose to play.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Development Safety Rules

The game will be built in small testable pieces.

No full script rewrites unless approved first.

Every code change must explain:

- What file is being changed
- What section is being changed
- Why it is being changed
- How to test it afterward

Before major changes, a backup or checkpoint must be created.

The project should always have a working version to return to.

New systems should be expandable instead of temporary throwaway code.

The first playable version will be small, but built as the foundation for the larger game.
