# Citizen and Memory System

## Purpose

The Citizen and Memory System makes the city feel alive.

The goal is not to simulate thousands of perfect people in Version 1.

The goal is to create a small group of persistent citizens who the game remembers over time.

The player should feel like people, vehicles, places, and past events matter.

---

## Version 1 Scope

Version 1 should use:

* 25 important persistent citizens
* 50 to 100 simple background civilians
* 10 to 20 vehicles
* 1 small district
* 1 police station
* 8 call types
* Short MDT history summaries
* Simple citizen routines
* No live AI required during gameplay

This keeps the game realistic for limited hardware while still proving the core idea.

---

## Important Citizens

Important citizens are persistent.

The game remembers them across shifts and future encounters.

Important citizens may include:

* Repeat offenders
* Store owners
* Victims
* Witnesses
* Regular callers
* People tied to traffic stops
* People tied to dispatch calls
* People connected to future investigations

---

## Background Civilians

Background civilians exist to make the world feel populated.

They do not need full memory or deep histories in Version 1.

They may walk, drive, shop, or appear in public areas.

Background civilians can become important later if the game needs to remember them.

---

## Citizen Data

Each important citizen should store:

* Name
* Age
* Home area
* Job
* Vehicle
* Criminal history
* Prior police contact
* Relationship with player
* Known warnings
* Known citations
* Known arrests
* Active warrants
* Important notes

Example:

Marcus Reed

Prior Contact:

Stopped by Officer Hoover for speeding.

Warning issued.

Cooperative.

Current Note:

Vehicle later seen near burglary area.

---

## Simple Routines

Important citizens should have simple routines.

Example:

Home → Work → Store → Home

Version 1 should not simulate every tiny life detail.

The game does not need to track:

* Brushing teeth
* Eating every meal
* Texting friends
* Exact emotional state every minute
* Full family trees
* Complex social schedules

Simple routines are enough for Version 1.

---

## MDT History

The MDT should show short useful history.

It should not show huge walls of text.

Example:

Prior Contacts:

* Speeding warning, 3 days ago
* Suspicious person call near Oak Street, 2 weeks ago

Officer Safety:

* Previously fled from traffic stop

The MDT should help the player remember important information without forcing them to memorize every past interaction.

---

## Citizen Memory of Player

Citizens remember the player after meaningful interactions.

Examples of meaningful interactions:

* Arrest
* Warning
* Citation
* Search
* Pursuit
* Help during emergency
* Repeated calls to same location
* Respectful treatment
* Bad treatment

Citizens do not need to remember every tiny conversation.

Memory should matter when it affects future behavior.

Example:

A citizen who was treated respectfully may cooperate more later.

A citizen who was arrested by the player may be nervous, angry, or more likely to flee.

---

## Vehicles and Memory

Vehicles should also be remembered.

A vehicle may have history such as:

* Prior traffic stops
* Prior warnings
* Prior citations
* Fled from police
* Linked to suspicious person call
* Linked to burglary area
* Stolen vehicle report
* Expired registration
* Insurance issues

When the player runs a plate, the MDT should surface the most relevant history.

---

## Location Memory

Locations should remember important events.

Example:

A house may have:

* 3 noise complaints in one week
* 1 domestic disturbance last month
* 1 welfare check last year

The MDT should flag repeat locations when relevant.

This makes the city feel like it has history.

---

## AI Use

Version 1 should not rely on live AI during gameplay.

Live AI could be expensive, slow, inconsistent, or too hard to run on limited hardware.

Instead:

AI can help create content during development.

Examples:

* Citizen names
* Citizen profiles
* Dialogue variations
* Call variations
* Business names
* Vehicle histories
* Criminal histories

The game itself should run using saved data and normal code.

---

## Core Rule

The city should remember meaningful events.

The player should not need to remember everything.

The MDT, dispatch, and call history should help the player understand why a person, vehicle, or location matters.
