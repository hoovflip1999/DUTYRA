# Version 1 District

## Purpose

The Version 1 district is the first playable patrol area.

It should be small enough to build and run on limited hardware, but detailed enough to test the core gameplay loop.

The district should support:

* Patrol driving
* Walking
* Traffic stops
* Dispatch calls
* Citizen memory
* Repeat locations
* Police station gameplay
* Basic interiors
* Reports
* Vehicle interactions

The goal is not to build the full city yet.

The goal is to build one strong district that can expand later.

---

## District Name

The first district is called:

Cedar Heights District

This is a fictional Midwest-inspired suburban city district.

The city can later expand district by district.

Future districts may include:

* Downtown
* Industrial area
* Highway zone
* Rural county
* Airport or transit area
* Parks or outskirts

---

## Map Style

Cedar Heights should feel like a clean, safe suburban district.

This does not mean nothing bad happens.

It means the area is mostly normal, with calls that can still become serious depending on the situation.

The district should include:

* Neighborhood streets
* Stores
* Apartments
* Parking lots
* A park
* A small office building
* A faster road for traffic enforcement
* A police station near the center

---

## Police Station Location

The police station should be near the center of the district.

Reason:

* Easy access to all calls
* Good for testing
* Short travel time
* Makes the first version easier to play
* Lets the player return for reports, gear, and shift changes without long drives

---

## Police Station Interior

The police station should be small but walkable.

Version 1 police station areas:

* Lobby
* Front desk
* Locker room
* Briefing room
* Report area
* Armory / gear room
* Holding area
* Garage / parking lot

The station should not be huge in Version 1.

It should only include what supports gameplay.

---

## Building Access Rule

The game should not make every single building fully enterable in Version 1.

Instead, use a balanced system.

Main public buildings should be enterable.

Some homes and offices should be enterable.

Background buildings can be exterior-only.

This gives the world a realistic feeling without making the project too large.

---

## Enterable Buildings

Version 1 should include these enterable or partially enterable locations:

* Police station — enterable
* Gas station / convenience store — enterable
* Grocery or retail store — enterable
* Diner or small restaurant — enterable
* Apartment building — lobby, hallways, and selected units
* About 50% of normal homes — enterable
* Small office building — first floor only
* Community center or small school — main areas only
* Park restroom or shelter building — enterable

---

## Background Buildings

Some buildings can be exterior-only in Version 1.

Examples:

* Extra houses
* Closed offices
* Decorative storefronts
* Buildings not tied to calls yet
* Buildings outside the active patrol area

These can be made enterable later if needed.

---

## Roads

The district should include:

* One main road
* One faster road or small highway edge
* Several neighborhood streets
* A few alleys or back streets
* Parking lots
* Driveways
* Sidewalks

The faster road supports:

* Speeding
* DUI calls
* Traffic enforcement
* Minor crashes
* Pursuits

Alleys and back streets support:

* Suspicious person calls
* Foot chases
* Vehicle pursuits
* Hidden vehicles
* Repeat problem locations

---

## Apartment Building

The apartment building should be one of the main repeat-call locations.

It can support:

* Noise complaints
* Domestic disturbances
* Welfare checks
* Suspicious person calls
* Medical calls
* Repeat location history

The MDT should be able to flag the apartment building if repeated calls happen there.

---

## Version 1 Building List

Recommended Version 1 building count:

* 1 police station
* 1 gas station / convenience store
* 1 grocery or retail store
* 1 diner or restaurant
* 1 apartment building
* 6 to 8 houses
* 1 small office building
* 1 community center or small school
* 1 park restroom or shelter building
* Several exterior-only background buildings

This keeps the district manageable while still giving enough places for calls.

---

## Expansion Rule

The city should expand district by district.

Do not build the full city first.

Build Cedar Heights first.

Once Cedar Heights works, future districts can be added around it.

Each new district should reuse existing systems instead of requiring everything to be rebuilt.

---

## Core Rule

Version 1 should feel small but real.

The player should be able to patrol the district, learn the streets, recognize repeat locations, and feel like the area has history.

The district should prove the main idea:

The city remembers, and every shift can play differently.
